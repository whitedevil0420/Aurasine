Why the wave shape matters

Modified square wave inverters approximate grid power in rough steps. Motors run hotter and noisier, laptop and phone chargers lose efficiency, and sensitive electronics — routers, medical equipment, audio gear — degrade faster.

AuraSine's pure sine wave output matches utility power precisely, so every appliance in the house runs exactly as it would on grid electricity.
